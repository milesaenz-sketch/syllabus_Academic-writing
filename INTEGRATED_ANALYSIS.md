## 3. The remaining discrepancy — the Phase 3 / Phase 4 boundary

The guides and the OVA/VLO scripts form one internally consistent set that splits the Phase 3–4 content differently from the syllabus:

- **Guides + OVAs:** Phase 3 = source integration **+ verification / tool disclosure** (all of LO2); Phase 4 = evaluation of the research argument (all of LO3).
- **Syllabus v2:** Phase 3 = source integration only (LO2a); Phase 4 = **verification / tool disclosure (LO2b) + evaluation of the research argument (LO3)**.

Both arrangements cover the same three content blocks (LO2 integration, LO2 verification/disclosure, LO3 argument) across Phases 3–4 and both total 250 pts there; they differ only in **where the verification block sits**, and therefore in the **phase titles**, the **VLO titles**, and the **LO tag of Phase 4** (guide: LO3 only; syllabus: LO2 and 3).

This is a genuine coordination item, not a wording slip: the guide for Phase 4 contains no source-verification / tool-disclosure quiz content, yet the syllabus's Phase 4 "Learning Evidence" and "Summative Assessment" require exactly that (alongside argument evaluation). A student or a gradebook keyed to the syllabus would expect verification to be assessed in Phase 4, while the guides assess it in Phase 3.

**Resolving it is a structural choice, so it is left to academic coordination.** Two coherent directions:

- **(A) Align the guides to the syllabus** (recommended if the syllabus is the fixed anchor): rename Guide 3 → "Appraisal of Source Integration in a Research Report"; move the verification / tool-disclosure content from Guide 3 into Guide 4; rename Guide 4 → "Verification of Sources and Evaluation of the Research Argument" and tag it LO2 and 3; re-title the Phase 3 and Phase 4 VLOs to match. This is a content rewrite of Guides 3–4 (and the OVA3/OVA4 scripts), **not** a form edit, so it was **not** applied here.
- **(B) Keep the guides' split and revise the syllabus's Phase 3–4 names and LO tags** to match them. This changes the syllabus and so is outside the stated constraint; noted only for completeness.

## 4. Form-level corrections applied to the guides

These are surface/wording fixes that do not touch the syllabus structure. Both were verified against the syllabus.

### 4.1 Guide 2 — declared learning outcome (applied)
The "Detailed description of the Phase" stated the verbatim Course **LO1** text ("To analyze the principal genres of academic and scientific production…"), identical to Guide 1, i.e. genre *identification* rather than the rhetorical structure that is the actual focus of Phase 2.

- **Changed to:** *"To analyze the rhetorical plan of the research article, explaining how its IMRaD structure and the rhetorical moves of each section respond to the rhetorical situation, the intended readership, and the conventions of the genre."*
- **Why it is sound:** it matches the Phase 2 body and the syllabus's Phase 2 *Learning Evidence* ("analyzes the rhetorical plan of the research article, determining the function that each section … performs within the genre and the readership it addresses").
- **Noted nuance:** the syllabus maps **both** Phase 1 and Phase 2 to Course LO1, so repeating the LO1 text was defensible; stating a phase-specific outcome is nonetheless clearer and is what the evaluator recommended (priority: *Important*).

### 4.2 Guide 1 — document the reused reading Jourdan et al. (2023) (applied)
Jourdan et al. (2023) appears among the four genre-classification stimulus texts in Phase 1, but the syllabus lists it as an official **LO3** resource. The evaluator asked that, if its use in Phase 1 is intentional, it be documented explicitly (as other cross-phase reuses are).

- **Added** (in the "Texts supplied for this phase" paragraph): a sentence stating that Jourdan et al. (2023) is an LO3 reading **intentionally** included in Phase 1 as a present-day exemplar of the preprint / working-paper genre to classify — not as theoretical reading for this phase — signposted the same way reuse is signposted in the later phases.
- **Why it is sound:** the syllabus confirms Jourdan et al. (2023) is an LO3 resource, so framing it as a deliberately reused stimulus (rather than a misfiled reading) is accurate.

### 4.3 Guides not changed
- **Guide 3, Guide 5:** no form defect found; internally coherent and (for Phases 1/2/5) aligned with the syllabus. Any change to Guide 3 would be the structural (A) decision in §3, not a form edit.
- **Guide 4:** "Intermediate" is correct (see §1); no change.
